Spotifast 0.17.2 polishes Lucid, Spicy Lyrics and the Now playing column. Album art now animates between songs and is cropped correctly in every cover slot, while the player, lyrics and panel controls show clear hover, keyboard focus and pressed states.

## Changed

- **The Now playing column follows Spotify's view more closely.** It is titled Now playing, puts Next in queue above About the artist, keeps track lengths in the queue, and scales its type for a clearer playing or paused hierarchy.
- **Lucid's expanded rail docks the playing cover.** The cover crops to the rail, and the empty, light, dark and narrow layouts keep their spacing with real artwork.

## Fixed

- **Cover changes crossfade instead of cutting.** The previous cover stays underneath while the new one fades in over 240 ms.
- **A corrupt or off-centre cover degrades gracefully.** Bad image bytes fall back to the placeholder, and wide or tall images are centre-cropped in every square art slot.
